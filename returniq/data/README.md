# Data

## Source

**UCI Machine Learning Repository — Online Retail II** (Chen, 2012).
<https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii> · License: **CC BY 4.0**.

Transactions of a UK-based online gift-ware retailer (many customers are wholesalers) from
**2009-12-01 to 2011-12-09**: 1,067,371 rows across two Excel sheets.

Reproduce the download (≈46 MB zip, not committed to git):

```bash
python scripts/download_data.py      # -> data/raw/online_retail_II.xlsx
```

`data/raw/`, `data/interim/` and `data/processed/` are git-ignored; every file in them is regenerated by `make pipeline`.

## Why this dataset (and what was considered)

ReturnIQ needs real transaction-level data with some outcome signal. Online Retail II was chosen because it is
free, permissively licensed, large enough to model (≈1M rows), and contains **credit-note (cancellation) invoices**.
Other public e-commerce datasets either have no outcome signal (e.g. clickstream sets), are heavily
anonymised/synthetic, or carry review/return data without transaction detail. The trade-off is below: this dataset
does **not** contain a return flag.

## Fields used

| Field | Used for |
|---|---|
| `Invoice` | order id; a leading `C` marks a credit note (cancellation) — used only to build labels and historical rates |
| `StockCode`, `Description` | product identity and display |
| `Quantity`, `Price` | quantity, unit price, line value |
| `InvoiceDate` | chronology, calendar features, point-in-time history, splits |
| `Customer ID` | customer history features and credit-note matching |
| `Country` | domestic flag (UK vs other) and filtering |

## Target definition (what is, and is not, a "return")

The data contains **no return flag, no reason code, and no link from a credit note to its original invoice**.
Credit notes (`Invoice` starting with `C`, negative `Quantity`) can be physical returns, order cancellations, or billing/price corrections.
ReturnIQ therefore never says "return" for a measured outcome. The label is:

> `y = 1` if a **credit note is matched to this purchase line within 30 days** of purchase.

Matching (`ml/data_prep.py::match_cancellations`): for each credit-note line, consume its quantity from the most recent earlier
purchase lines of the **same customer and same product** that still have unreturned units. A line is positive if any of its units
were consumed by a credit note dated ≤ 30 days after the purchase.

Validation of the matching on this run: **16,003 of 17,586 (91.0%)** modelled credit-note rows and **450,259 of 464,292 (97.0%)** credited units match an earlier purchase.
Of all matched lines, 75% have their first credit note within 30 days (median lag ≈ 10 days), which is why 30 days is the label window.

Resulting label: **11,321 positive lines of 712,562 observable lines (1.59%)**.

## Cleaning (all counts from `reports/data_prep_stats.json`)

| Step | Rows |
|---|---|
| Raw rows (both sheets) | 1,067,371 |
| Exact duplicates removed (the two sheets overlap on 2010-12-01..09) | 34,335 |
| Non-product stock codes removed (`POST`, `M`, `D`, `BANK CHARGES`, …) | 5,980 |
| Non-credit rows with negative quantity (stock adjustments) dropped | 3,393 |
| Non-credit rows with price ≤ 0 dropped | 6,019 |
| Purchase rows without `Customer ID` (cannot be linked or modelled) dropped | 226,637 |
| **Purchase lines modelled** | **776,577** |
| Credit-note product lines modelled | 17,586 |

Right-censoring: purchases in the last 30 days of the data (2011-11-10 onward) have an unfinished outcome window. They are **not labelled**; the app shows them as "open".

## Leakage analysis

See `reports/eda_summary.json → leakage_audit` (generated by `python -m ml.eda`). In short:
the `C` invoice prefix, negative quantities, matched-credit fields, invoice numbers and raw customer/product IDs are never model inputs;
historical credit-note rates are computed **point-in-time** (only credit notes dated strictly before the purchase);
train/validation/test are **chronological** with a 30-day purge gap so no training label depends on events in a later split.

| Split | Purchase dates | Lines | Positive rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Train | 2009-12-01 → 2011-03-31 | 478,272 | 1.58% |
| Validation | 2011-05-01 → 2011-06-30 | 54,780 | 1.79% |
| Test | 2011-07-31 → 2011-11-09 | 131,932 | 1.56% |
| Open (unlabelled) | 2011-11-09 → 2011-12-09 | 64,015 | — |

## Limitations of the data

* Target is a credit note, not a confirmed return; reasons and exchanges are not observable.
* Matching of credit notes to purchases is heuristic.
* ~22% of purchase rows lack a customer id and are excluded.
* Wholesale gift-ware, mostly UK customers; no size, category, review or margin data.
* The window begins in Dec 2009, so "new customer" means "first seen in the data", and early-period history features are sparse.
