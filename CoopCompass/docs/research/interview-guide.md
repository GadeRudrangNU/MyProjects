# Interview guide (30 minutes)

**Status: guide only. No interviews have been conducted.**

Goal: understand real workflow, decision criteria and trust, then test reactions to concepts. Listen more than you talk; ask for specific recent examples, not opinions about hypotheticals.

**Consent script (read aloud):** "I'm researching how students search for co-ops. I'll take notes, not record, unless you agree. Nothing will be attributed to you by name, and you can skip any question or stop at any time."

## 0-3 min Warm-up
- Tell me about your current search: what are you looking for, and how's it going?

## 3-10 min Current workflow
- Walk me through the last posting you applied to, from finding it to submitting. (Show me if you can.)
- Which tools/tabs/documents were involved? Where did you write things down?
- How long did that application take end to end? How many postings did you open that day, and how many did you apply to?

## 10-14 min Frustrations and workarounds
- What was the most annoying or time-consuming step? What do you do to cope (templates, spreadsheets, AI tools)?
- Tell me about a time you applied and later felt it was a waste of time. What would have told you earlier?

## 14-18 min Decision criteria and constraints
- How do you decide a posting is worth applying to? What makes you skip one in seconds?
- Role / location / work-authorization / sponsorship constraints: how do they shape what you consider? How do you find out if a company sponsors?
- Quality vs. quantity: how many applications a week, and have you changed that approach?

## 18-22 min Trust and privacy
- Have you used AI for job applications? What worked, what didn't? Anything it made up?
- If a tool gave a job a "78% match", what would you want to see before believing it?
- How do you feel about uploading your resume to a tool? What would you need to see/know (where stored, what is sent to AI)?

## 22-28 min Concept reactions (show low-fidelity screens, one at a time)
1. Ranked list with a match score only. What would you do with it? Do you trust it?
2. The same job with factor breakdown + evidence from your resume. Better/worse? Why?
3. Resume gap view (present / weak / no evidence). Useful? What's missing?
4. AI rewrite of a bullet that shows its source evidence. Would you use it? What would worry you?
5. Tracker with outcome analytics. Would you keep it updated? What would make you stop?
- If you could only keep one of these, which and why?

## 28-30 min Wrap-up
- What did I not ask that I should have? Anyone else I should talk to?

## After the interview
Write notes within an hour: verbatim quotes, observed workarounds, surprises, and which hypothesis (H1-H5) each supports or contradicts. De-identify before storing. Code themes in `research-findings-template.md`.
